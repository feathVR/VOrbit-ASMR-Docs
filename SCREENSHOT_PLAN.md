The harness does not open audio devices or change the user's saved settings. All included images were visually checked for personal information, secrets, and device-specific names.

## Capture method

The current audio images came from `artifacts/docs-site-current-ui`, generated by the app's own offscreen WPF control test. No UI was fabricated or edited. The image may show a development build path; the VST bridge page explicitly identifies it as an example and tells readers to use the path shown by their own installation.
